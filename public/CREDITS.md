## Food composition

USDA Standard Reference 28 (2015), public nutritional reference values, obtained through the [maxsu/USDA-SR28 text mirror](https://github.com/maxsu/USDA-SR28). The mirror does not declare a separate software license; no endorsement is implied. Values are per 100 g edible portion, not measurements of a user's actual food. Source IDs: 09040, 01132, 01256, 05064, 08121, 08120, 16015, 20045, 18075, 11091, 11508, 15121, 16070, 01012, 04053. Household weights come from the same reference. SR28 is historical; current labels and preparation methods may differ.

Open Food Facts results are manufacturer/community-contributed data under the source's database license. The product displays provenance and does not turn missing nutrients into measured zeroes.
